# Marketing & Business Analyst Workflow

Use this workflow when you need marketing research, business analysis, campaign strategy, performance reporting, or competitive intelligence.

---

## 1. Define Objective
Clarify the business or marketing goal:
- What problem are we solving or what opportunity are we pursuing?
- Who is the target audience or customer segment?
- What is the desired outcome (leads, revenue, brand awareness, retention, etc.)?

## 2. Market & Audience Research
- Identify target market segments (demographics, psychographics, behaviors)
- Analyze customer pain points and buying journey
- Review competitors: positioning, pricing, channels, messaging
- Gather data sources: surveys, Google Analytics, social insights, CRM data, industry reports

## 3. SWOT / PESTLE Analysis
- **SWOT**: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- **PESTLE**: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental factors affecting the business

## 4. Business Metrics & KPIs
Define and track key metrics:
- **Acquisition**: CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost), traffic, leads, conversion rate
- **Activation**: Onboarding rate, first purchase
- **Retention**: Churn rate, LTV (Lifetime Value), repeat purchase rate
- **Revenue**: MRR/ARR, AOV (Average Order Value), ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- **Referral**: NPS, referral rate

## 5. Campaign Strategy
- Define channels (SEO, paid ads, social media, email, content, influencer, offline)
- Set campaign objectives and budget allocation
- Craft messaging and value proposition per segment
- Plan content calendar and creative assets

## 6. Data Analysis & Reporting
- Pull data from relevant platforms (GA4, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopee/Tokopedia dashboards, etc.)
- Build performance dashboards or summary tables
- Identify trends, anomalies, and root causes
- Compare against benchmarks and previous periods

## 7. Insights & Recommendations
- Summarize key findings in plain language
- Prioritize actions by impact vs. effort (2x2 matrix)
- Provide specific, actionable next steps
- Flag risks and dependencies

## 8. Reporting Output Format
Structure final reports as:
1. **Executive Summary** — 3–5 bullet highlights
2. **Performance Overview** — tables/charts of KPIs
3. **Analysis** — what's working, what's not, why
4. **Recommendations** — ranked action list
5. **Next Steps** — owners, timelines, success criteria

---

## Example Prompts to Trigger This Workflow
- "Analyze our Instagram campaign performance for March"
- "Write a competitive analysis for our product vs. [competitor]"
- "What marketing channels should we prioritize for Q3?"
- "Create a business case for launching a new product line"
- "Summarize our monthly sales report and give recommendations"
